Biography

About Me & Research Focus

I am a Cybersecurity Researcher and PhD Candidate in the School of Computer Science at the Queensland University of Technology (QUT) in Brisbane, Australia, and a member of the QUT Centre for Data Science. Concurrently, I serve as a Lecturer in Information Technology at the Asia Pacific International College (APIC), an Academic Team Member at the Higher Education Leadership Institute (HELI), and a Sessional Academic at QUT. Previously, I served as the Principal Cyber Security Advisor at SECBLOK (November 2024 – May 2025).

My academic trajectory bridges a strong theoretical foundation in Mathematics (BSc, Mustansiriyah University) and Data Security (High Diploma, UoITC) with applied advanced cybersecurity engineering (Master of Information Technology in Cybersecurity, QUT). In 2025, I was honored with an Associate Fellowship of Advance HE (AFHEA) in recognition of my commitment to educational quality and higher education pedagogy.

My research lies at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, and Software Safety. I investigate how machine learning and foundation models can be rigorously evaluated, made trustworthy, and deployed for automated vulnerability detection and proactive defense in high-assurance environments.

AI & Multi-LLM Security

Automated vulnerability discovery, LLM zero-shot and few-shot reasoning, ensemble architectures, and empirical benchmark evaluation.

Software & Network Security

Proactive threat mitigation, penetration testing frameworks, static code analysis, and CVE severity prediction.

Trustworthy & Explainable AI

Addressing data scarcity, bias mitigation, explainable AI (XAI), and robustness requirements in safety-critical deployments.

Cryptanalysis & Digital Forensics

Block cipher cryptanalysis, mobile device forensics (Android), and connected & autonomous vehicle threat modeling.

Specialization

Research Focus & Core Themes

Academic focus areas across cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, and software safety

AI & Foundation Models in Software Security

Core Focus

Investigating deep neural networks, large language models, and automated reasoning techniques for identifying vulnerabilities in complex software codebases, evaluating zero-shot detection reliability, and mitigating prompt and output hallucinations in safety-critical audit pipelines.

Vulnerability Auditing LLM Reasoning Code Analysis Static Verification

Trustworthy, Robust & Explainable AI

Core Focus

Developing methodological criteria and algorithmic techniques to overcome critical machine learning challenges, notably data scarcity, adversarial vulnerabilities, and interpretability in critical infrastructure and healthcare informatics.

Data Scarcity Explainable AI (XAI) Trustworthy AI Model Regularization

Mobile & IoT Device Digital Forensics

Core Focus

Empirical forensic investigation of mobile operating systems, Android file systems, and wearable IoT devices (such as Fitbit hardware), analyzing data storage structures, evidence acquisition methodologies, and cryptographic data recovery protocols.

Android Forensics IoT Security Storage Forensics Data Carving

Applied Cryptography & Cryptanalysis

Core Focus

Theoretical exploration and algorithmic implementation of cryptanalysis methods spanning classical ciphers, Vigenère, stream ciphers, and block cipher architectures, assessing computational complexity and cryptographic resilience.

Block Ciphers Stream Ciphers Cryptanalysis Mathematical Cryptography
