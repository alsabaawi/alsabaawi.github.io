Biography

About Me & Research Focus

I am a Cybersecurity Researcher and PhD Candidate at the Queensland University of Technology (QUT) in Brisbane, Australia, affiliated with the QUT Centre for Data Science. Concurrently, I serve as a Lecturer in Information Technology at the Asia Pacific International College (APIC), a Sessional Academic at QUT, and the Principal Cyber Security Advisor at SECBLOK.

My academic trajectory bridges a strong theoretical foundation in Mathematics (BSc, Mustansiriyah University) and Data Security (High Diploma, UoITC) with applied advanced cybersecurity engineering (Master of Information Technology in Cybersecurity, QUT). In 2025, I was honored with an Associate Fellowship of Advance HE (AFHEA) in recognition of my commitment to educational quality and higher education pedagogy.

My research lies at the intersection of Large Language Models (LLMs), automated software vulnerability detection, and trustworthy AI security. I develop high-assurance frameworks (such as AutoResearch-Sec and X-SecEnsemble) and rigorous benchmark suites across thousands of real-world and synthetic vulnerabilities (JavaVulBench, NIST Juliet, and Project-KB) to evaluate, explain, and advance model performance in mission-critical environments.

AI & Multi-LLM Security

Automated vulnerability discovery, LLM zero-shot and few-shot reasoning, ensemble architectures, and empirical benchmark evaluation.

Software & Network Security

Proactive threat mitigation, penetration testing frameworks, static code analysis, and CVE severity prediction.

Trustworthy & Explainable AI

Addressing data scarcity, bias mitigation, explainable AI (XAI), and robustness requirements in safety-critical deployments.

Cryptanalysis & Digital Forensics

Block cipher cryptanalysis, mobile device forensics (Android), and connected & autonomous vehicle threat modeling.
